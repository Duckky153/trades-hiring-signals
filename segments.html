Assumptions

  • Seasonality, as an assumption: HVAC demand peaks in summer and winter, and plumbing is steadier through the year. A late-September snapshot may catch HVAC shops between peaks, hiring for the winter rush. This page doesn't adjust for that.
  • A segment with one or two shops can rank high on a single good shop. Treat small segments as leads, not patterns.
  • Electrical, roofing and pest are adjacent trades: they write estimates too, but the follow-up work and the software differ. Use the toggle to leave them out.
  • Sun Belt here means Phoenix, Dallas, Tampa and Atlanta; Midwest means Columbus and Chicago; Northeast means Philadelphia and Northern New Jersey.
  • Hiring is not buying. A segment full of follow-up hires shows where the work is piling up, not who will buy.
